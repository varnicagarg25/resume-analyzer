Special Needs Program Coordinator (Fort Collins, CO)

Seeking a dedicated coordinator with a Bachelor's degree in Early Childhood Development or Elementary Education and 4+ years of experience caring for special needs children and adults. You will oversee daily youth activities, determine adult work placements, manage client records, coordinate healthcare and financial resources for families, and supervise part-time counselors and volunteers.

Key Responsibilities:
- Determine work placements for special needs adult clients and maintain client databases.
- Plan and oversee daily youth activities, classroom support, and community outings.
- Coordinate client contact with local healthcare professionals and assist families with financial assistance.
- Supervise part-time counselors and volunteer workers.
